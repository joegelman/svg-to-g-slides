# Classroom visuals, made in Google Slides

Recolour clipart to match your room, make the shapes the menu doesn't have, put a student photo in any shape, and change one colour across a whole template. SVG to Slides is a free Chrome extension. It converts files inside your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

_Free forever. Every feature, no daily cap, no account, no Pro tier._ Install: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/svg-to-slides/bdhhnakellmdnbgbdokiojipaebdeioi

## Recolour clipart to your class colours
Clipart often comes as an SVG file. Drag the .svg onto a slide and every part arrives as its own Slides shape. Select one piece, recolour it with the regular fill picker, and resize it without losing quality.
Check your clipart's terms first: some artists allow editable or moveable pieces only with an extended license.

- Recolouring SVGs in Google Slides: https://svgslid.es/guides/recolor-svg-in-google-slides
- Inserting an SVG, step by step: https://svgslid.es/guides/insert-svg-into-google-slides

## Make the shapes the menu doesn't have
Draw a shape point by point with the pen, or build one from shapes you already have: Union joins them, Subtract cuts one out of another. To reshape anything later, double-click it and click Edit Shape.

- Pen tool for Google Slides: https://svgslid.es/guides/pen-tool-google-slides
- Combine shapes in Google Slides: https://svgslid.es/guides/combine-shapes-google-slides
- Edit points in Google Slides: https://svgslid.es/guides/edit-points-google-slides

## Put a photo in any shape
Put a shape over a picture, select both, and mask. Any shape or group of shapes can be the mask, including letters you have outlined, so a student photo can sit inside a star or the first letter of their name. Double-click the result to move the photo under the shape.

- Masking an image with a shape: https://svgslid.es/docs/mask-image
- Outlining text: https://svgslid.es/docs/outline-stroke-text

## Change one colour across a whole template
With nothing selected, open Replace a colour, pick the colour to change and the new one, and tick All slides. Fills, strokes, gradient stops and text change, inside groups too, and one Command+Z undoes the lot.

- Replace a colour across Google Slides: https://svgslid.es/guides/replace-color-google-slides

## For your school's IT team

- Google account access: None. The extension asks for no Google sign-in and no Google permission. It cannot read Drive or reach presentations through Google's API.
- Chrome permissions: Version 1.4.0: access to Google Slides pages, where its tools run, and storage, to remember your settings. No access to other sites.
- Files: Converted inside the browser. Nothing is uploaded.
- Network: One kind of request. Outline text downloads its font from Google Fonts. The request names the font family, weight and style, is sent without cookies, and carries no text, files or slide content.
- Stored on the computer: Whether the toolbar and Layers are open, snapping settings, layer names, recent colours, the original text of outlined text, the original outline of shapes whose stroke was moved, and whether the update note was shown. Not synced. Removed with the extension.
- Tracking: None in the extension. No account.
- Chrome Web Store ID: bdhhnakellmdnbgbdokiojipaebdeioi, for an allowlist or force-install in the Google Admin console.

The web uploader and the Workspace add-on send files to the svgslid.es server to convert. The extension uploads nothing.
