# SVG logos and icons in Google Slides™ themes

Convert your logo or icon set with SVG to Slides, add it to your theme layouts, and reuse it on every slide. The shapes resize without losing quality and also work in Google Drawings.

_Free forever. Every feature, no daily cap, no account, no Pro tier._

- Install the Chrome extension: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/svg-to-slides/bdhhnakellmdnbgbdokiojipaebdeioi
- Online converter: https://svgslid.es/convert

## What you can do
- **Use SVGs in your themes.** Put converted logos and icons in theme layouts so they appear on every slide that uses them.
- **Resize without losing quality.** From a small footer icon to a full-slide graphic, every edge stays sharp.
- **Build an icon library.** Upload a batch at svgslid.es/convert and get one slide per icon, ready to copy from.
- **Works in Google Drawings.** The native shapes also work in Google Drawings.

## Questions

### Can I put an SVG logo in a Google Slides theme?
Yes. Convert it with SVG to Slides, then add it to your theme layouts. It stays a native, resizable shape.

### How do I convert a whole icon set?
Upload the files at svgslid.es/convert. You get a Google Drive link to a file with one slide per SVG.

### Will my logo stay sharp when I scale it?
Yes. Converted shapes are vectors, so they resize at any size and stay crisp.
