# SVG to Slides blog

Short, design-minded notes on Google Slides and Docs.

- [Product screenshots go stale. Native UI mockups don't.](https://svgslid.es/blog/native-ui-mockups-google-slides.md) (2026-11-10): Every release breaks the screenshots in your decks. Build simplified product UI as native shapes and live text instead, kept in one library deck, and a renamed button is a find and replace.
- [Run a messaging workshop in Google Forms and build a brand style sheet in Google Sheets](https://svgslid.es/blog/messaging-workshop-brand-style-sheet-google-sheets.md) (2026-11-09): A form collects the answers. One sheet turns them into messaging. A second sheet holds the brand's colours, type and voice in a form scripts and Gems can read.
- [Update Google Slides collateral from one messaging sheet with Gemini](https://svgslid.es/blog/google-slides-collateral-messaging-sheet-gemini.md) (2026-11-09): Write the brief once in a sheet. Gemini writes each line to a recipe and a character limit, and a script puts it in every one-pager, brief, whitepaper and battlecard.
- [Google Slides finally does letter spacing](https://svgslid.es/blog/letter-spacing-google-slides.md) (2026-11-07): Tracking has landed in Slides. Here's what it is, how to use it well, and why Docs looks next.
- [Audit what Gemini's whole-deck edit changed](https://svgslid.es/blog/audit-gemini-whole-deck-edits-google-slides.md) (2026-11-03): Gemini can now edit an entire presentation from one prompt. It tells you what it changed. Here is how to check, object by object, with a snapshot before and a diff after.
- [A competitor matrix where every cell cites its source](https://svgslid.es/blog/competitor-matrix-google-sheets-gemini-citations.md) (2026-10-29): AI-filled competitive research was unusable because nobody could check it. As of this week, Fill with Gemini attaches a web source to each cell. Build the matrix around that.
- [On-brand charts in Slides, from Sheets data to native shapes](https://svgslid.es/blog/on-brand-charts-google-slides.md) (2026-10-26): Sheets keeps adding chart types. None of them look like your brand once they're in a deck. Draw the chart as shapes instead, and every bar takes your colours and type.
- [Workspace Studio won't run on shared files. Design around it.](https://svgslid.es/blog/workspace-studio-shared-files-messaging-flow.md) (2026-10-13): Studio flows fail on shared drives, shared folders and IMPORTRANGE. Messaging sheets are shared by definition. Here is the two-sheet pattern that works.
- [Turn your messaging recipes into Gemini skills](https://svgslid.es/blog/gemini-skills-messaging-recipes.md) (2026-10-08): Skills are replacing Gems as the way to give Gemini standing instructions. Write your copy rules once and call them with @ in Docs, Slides and Gmail.
- [Customer proof points expire. Track them.](https://svgslid.es/blog/customer-proof-points-expiry-google-sheets.md) (2026-09-09): Logos, quotes and case studies come with permission, and permission has an end date. Keep a proof library in Sheets and get warned before a quote goes stale.
- [Find which slide draws the objections](https://svgslid.es/blog/google-meet-notes-screenshots-slide-objections.md) (2026-08-12): Meet notes now capture the slide on screen. Log each objection against that slide and the weak spots in your deck show up in a week.
- [Certify reps on the new message with a Forms quiz](https://svgslid.es/blog/sales-enablement-quiz-google-forms.md) (2026-08-04): A launch isn't landed until the people selling it can say it. Build a short quiz from the messaging, score it in Sheets, and follow up with whoever missed.
- [Localize a one-pager without breaking the layout](https://svgslid.es/blog/localize-one-pager-google-slides.md) (2026-07-31): Translations run long. Give every slot a budget per language, draft in Sheets, and catch the overflow before it reaches the page.
