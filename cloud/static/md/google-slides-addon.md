# SVG add-on for Google Slides

A Google Slides add-on that inserts SVG files as real, editable vector shapes. Get it from the Google Workspace Marketplace: https://svgslid.es/addon

## How it works
1. In Slides, choose Insert SVGs from the Extensions menu, or click the add-on's icon in the side panel.
2. Pick your SVG files. They convert as you select them. Click Copy or press Enter.
3. Press Cmd/Ctrl+V. The shapes land on the slide you have open.

## Good to know
- Select several files and they land side by side on the current slide.
- A gradient comes through as its first colour. For full gradients use the web uploader: https://svgslid.es/convert
- Free forever. Every feature, no daily cap, no account, no Pro tier.

## Other ways in
