# SVG to Slides guides

Featured: [Does Google Slides&trade; support SVG?](https://svgslid.es/guides/does-google-slides-support-svg.md)

## Start here
- [SVG vs PNG in Google Slides&trade;](https://svgslid.es/guides/svg-vs-png-google-slides.md): When Google Slides flattens an SVG it becomes a single image that blurs when resized and can't be recoloured or edited. SVG to Slides converts every path into Slides' own native shape format instead, so the result behaves like a shape you drew yourself.
- [How to insert an SVG into Google Slides&trade;](https://svgslid.es/guides/insert-svg-into-google-slides.md): Google Slides doesn't support SVG files, so you can't upload one as an editable graphic. To insert an SVG into Google Slides, install the free SVG to Slides Chrome extension, open your presentation, and drag the .svg file onto a slide. Each path becomes its own native Slides shape.
- [Does Google Slides&trade; support SVG?](https://svgslid.es/guides/does-google-slides-support-svg.md): No. Google Slides doesn't keep SVG files as editable vectors. Your options are to flatten the SVG into a PNG, or convert it through EMF and Google Drawings. SVG to Slides converts the file into Slides' native shape format, so it stays editable.

## Design workflows
- [SVG to Google Slides&trade; converter](https://svgslid.es/guides/svg-to-google-slides-converter.md): SVG to Slides converts SVG files into native, editable Google Slides shapes. Use the Chrome extension to drop files straight onto a slide, or the web converter to turn a batch of SVGs into a Slides-ready file in Google Drive. Every surface is free.
- [Edit and recolor SVGs in Google Slides&trade;](https://svgslid.es/guides/recolor-svg-in-google-slides.md): Once converted with SVG to Slides, every path in your SVG is a separate native Slides shape. Select one element and change its fill with the standard picker, edit its points, or animate it on its own.
- [SVG logos and icons in Google Slides&trade; themes](https://svgslid.es/guides/svg-logos-icons-google-slides.md): Convert your logo or icon set with SVG to Slides, add it to your theme layouts, and reuse it on every slide. The shapes resize without losing quality and also work in Google Drawings.

## Editing tools
- [How to edit points in Google Slides&trade;](https://svgslid.es/guides/edit-points-google-slides.md): Install SVG to Slides, double-click the shape and click Edit Shape. Drag a point to move it, click a point to show its curve handles, then click Done. The shape updates in place and keeps its place in the stack.
- [A pen tool for Google Slides&trade;](https://svgslid.es/guides/pen-tool-google-slides.md): Install SVG to Slides. With nothing selected, press Option+Shift+P (Alt+Shift+P on Windows) or click Pen on the toolbar. Click to place corners, drag for curves, click the first point to close the shape, then click Done. Each path becomes a native Slides shape.
- [How to combine shapes in Google Slides&trade;](https://svgslid.es/guides/combine-shapes-google-slides.md): Install SVG to Slides, select two or more shapes, and press Option+Shift+U for Union or Option+Shift+S for Subtract (Alt+Shift on Windows). Intersect and Exclude are Option+Shift+I and Option+Shift+E. The same four commands are in the Combine section of the toolbar.
- [How to replace a colour throughout Google Slides&trade;](https://svgslid.es/guides/replace-color-google-slides.md): Install SVG to Slides. Click an empty part of the slide and press Option+Shift+F (Alt+Shift+F on Windows). Pick the colour to change, set the new one, tick All slides for the whole deck, and click the tick. One Command+Z undoes the whole run.
