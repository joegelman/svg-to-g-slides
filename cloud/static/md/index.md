# SVG to Slides — editable SVGs in Google Slides

SVG to Slides is a free Chrome extension that inserts SVG files into Google Slides as native, editable shapes.

Free forever. Every feature, no daily cap, no account, no Pro tier.

- Install: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/svg-to-slides/bdhhnakellmdnbgbdokiojipaebdeioi
- Online converter (no install): https://svgslid.es/convert
- Google Slides add-on: https://svgslid.es/addon

## The problem
Google Slides doesn't support SVG files. Your options are to flatten them into PNGs or take the long way through EMF conversion, Google Drawings, and copy-paste.

## What it does
- **Edit individual elements.** Every SVG path becomes its own Slides shape. Recolour any element with the standard fill picker.
- **Resize without losing quality.** From a small footer icon to a full-slide graphic, every edge stays sharp.
- **Use SVGs in your themes.** Add converted graphics to theme layouts and reuse them across slides.
- **Edit any shape as SVG.** Double-click a shape and move its points and curve handles right on the slide. Works on Slides' own shapes, lines and WordArt too. (https://svgslid.es/whats-new)

## How it works
1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store. No account required.
2. Drag in your SVG and click where it goes. Its paths become editable shapes.
3. Double-click any shape to edit its points as SVG.

## Limits
- Gradients come through in the online converter; in the extension and add-on they currently collapse to a single solid colour.
- Arc commands (A/a) are converted into smooth curves.
- Up to 25 files per drop in the extension; use the online converter for larger batches.

## Questions
### Can you upload or import an SVG file into Google Slides?
Not as an editable graphic. Google Slides doesn't support SVG files. Install SVG to Slides and drag the file onto a slide, or convert it online at svgslid.es/convert. Every path arrives as a native Slides shape.

### How is this different from Microsoft PowerPoint?
PowerPoint can insert an SVG file and convert it to shapes. Google Slides can't. SVG to Slides gives Slides the same result: vector graphics you can select, recolour, and resize.

### Can you edit the points of a shape in Google Slides?
Only for lines and polylines you draw yourself. With SVG to Slides, double-click any shape, including Slides’ own shapes and WordArt, and click Edit Shape to move its points and curves in place. See how.

### Can I add SVG icons to Google Slides?
Yes. Drag up to 25 SVG icons onto a slide at once. Each one becomes editable shapes with no background box, so you can recolour it to match your theme.

### Do the shapes stay sharp when I resize them?
Yes. They're vector graphics, so a small footer icon and a full-slide graphic both keep crisp edges.

### Can I use SVG logos in my Google Slides theme?
Yes. Add converted logos and icons to your theme layouts and reuse them across slides. The shapes also work in Google Drawings.

### Is it free?
Yes. Free forever. Every feature, no daily cap, no account, no Pro tier.

## Privacy
The Chrome extension converts on your device with an embedded WebAssembly engine; your file usually never leaves the browser. Details: https://svgslid.es/privacy
