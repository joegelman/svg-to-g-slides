# How to combine shapes in Google Slides™

Install SVG to Slides, select two or more shapes, and press Option+Shift+U for Union or Option+Shift+S for Subtract (Alt+Shift on Windows). Intersect and Exclude are Option+Shift+I and Option+Shift+E. The same four commands are in the Combine section of the toolbar.

## How to do it
1. **Select the shapes.** Select two or more shapes on the slide. For Subtract, the bottom shape is the one that keeps its outline.
2. **Run the command.** Click it in the Combine section of the toolbar, or press its shortcut.
3. **Undo if you need to.** One Command+Z (Ctrl+Z on Windows) splits them again.

## The four commands

| Command | Result | Shortcut (Mac) |
|---|---|---|
| Union | One shape covering everything the selected shapes cover. | Option+Shift+U |
| Subtract | The bottom shape with the shapes above it cut out. | Option+Shift+S |
| Intersect | Only the area where the shapes overlap. | Option+Shift+I |
| Exclude | Everything except the area where the shapes overlap. | Option+Shift+E |

## Limits
- Gradients, pattern fills, transparency and shadows are not carried into the result.
- Images, video and charts cannot be combined. To cut a picture to a shape, use Mask image with shape.
- The shapes need a fill or an outline.
- Intersect needs shapes that overlap. If a command would leave nothing, the extension says so and changes nothing.
Inside Edit Shape the same buttons act on the shapes with selected points. Combining shapes reference.

## Questions

### How do I merge shapes in Google Slides?
Select them and use Union (Option+Shift+U). You get one shape covering everything the selected shapes covered.

### How do I cut one shape out of another?
Put the shape to cut on top, select both, and use Subtract (Option+Shift+S). The bottom shape keeps its outline, minus the shapes above it.

### Can I undo a combine?
Yes. One Command+Z splits the shapes again.
