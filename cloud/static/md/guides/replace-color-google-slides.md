# How to replace a colour throughout Google Slides™

Install SVG to Slides. Click an empty part of the slide and press Option+Shift+F (Alt+Shift+F on Windows). Pick the colour to change, set the new one, tick All slides for the whole deck, and click the tick. One Command+Z undoes the whole run.

- See every editing tool: https://svgslid.es/vector-editor

## How to do it
1. **Clear the selection.** Click an empty part of the slide so that nothing is selected.
2. **Open Replace a colour.** Press Option+Shift+F, or click the paint bucket on the toolbar.
3. **Pick the old colour.** Click it in the list, or pick it from the slide with the eyedropper.
4. **Set the new colour.** The slide shows it in every place the old colour is used.
5. **Choose the scope and apply.** Tick All slides to change it on every slide; the list then shows the colours of the whole presentation. Click the tick.

## What changes, and what is kept
- Fills, strokes, gradient stops and text are changed, inside groups too.
- A run across all slides shows a progress bar and is undone with one press of Command+Z.
- Most changes are made on the object itself, so its animation and link stay. When an object is replaced instead.

## Long presentations
- The colours of other slides are read from the filmstrip. Slides far down the filmstrip may not be listed until you scroll to them.

## Questions

### Does it change text colour too?
Yes. Fills, strokes, gradient stops and text all change.

### Does it reach shapes inside groups?
Yes.

### Can I see the result before I apply it?
Yes. While the panel is open, the slide shows the new colour in every place the old one is used. The cross puts the slide back.

### Can I undo it?
Yes. One Command+Z undoes the whole run, even across every slide.
