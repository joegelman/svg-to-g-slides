# Rounding corners

Applies to: SVG to Slides Chrome extension, versions 1.2.0 and 1.3.0.

In Edit Shape, shapes with corners show a small circle just inside each corner. Drag a circle toward the middle of the shape to round the corner, or type a radius in pixels.

**To round the corners of a shape**

1. Open the shape in Edit Shape. See [Edit Shape: points and curves](https://svgslid.es/docs/edit-shape.md).
2. Drag one of the small circles inside a corner toward the middle of the shape. A pill under the shape shows the radius in pixels.
3. To set an exact radius, type a value in the pill and press Enter.
4. Click **Done**.

![Three cards on a slide in Edit Shape, each with rounded corners and a circle inside every corner. A pill under them reads 25 px, All corners.](https://svgslid.es/static/docs/img/round-corners-1-2.jpg)
Figure 1. Three cards, twelve corners, one radius. Recorded in Google Slides with version 1.2.0.

## Which corners change

| Selection in the editor | Corners that round |
|---|---|
| The whole shape, or nothing | Every corner of the shape. |
| Several shapes | Every corner of every shape, together. |
| One point or a few points | Those corners only. |

Hold Shift while dragging to change the radius in steps of 10 pixels.

## Several shapes at once

**To give several shapes the same radius**

1. Select the shapes on the slide.
2. Open Edit Shape.
3. Type the radius in the pill and press Enter.
4. Click **Done**.
