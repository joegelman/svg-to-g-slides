# Workspace Add-on

Applies to: SVG to Slides Workspace Add-on for Google Slides.

The add-on inserts SVG files from a dialog inside Google Slides. Pick your files, copy, and paste. Every path lands as a native shape on the slide you have open.

**To insert SVG files with the add-on**

1. In Google Slides, choose **Insert SVGs** from the **Extensions** menu, or click the add-on's icon in the side panel.
2. Pick your .svg files. They convert as you select them.
3. Click **Copy**, or press Enter.
4. Press Command+V. The shapes land on the slide you have open.

![The add-on dialog titled Insert SVGs with four shape thumbnails, the line Ready, click below or press Enter to copy, and a Copy button](https://svgslid.es/static/docs/img/add-on-dialog.jpg)

## Good to know

- Select more than one file and they land side by side on the current slide.
- A gradient comes through as its first colour. For full gradients use the [Web Uploader](https://svgslid.es/docs/web-uploader.md).
- The add-on is installed from the Google Workspace Marketplace: [svgslid.es/addon](https://svgslid.es/addon).

## What the add-on can access

| Access | Used for |
|---|---|
| The current presentation's page size | Sizing the inserted shapes to fit the slide. |
| Showing a dialog | The **Insert SVGs** dialog. |
| Calling the svgslid.es converter | Converting the files you select. |

The add-on has no access to Google Drive and does not read or change the content of your presentation through Google's API. The shapes are written to your clipboard and you paste them. See [Permissions and data](https://svgslid.es/docs/permissions-privacy.md#add-on).
