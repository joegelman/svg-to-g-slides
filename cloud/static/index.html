Google Slides™ doesn’t support SVG files. Your options are to flatten them into PNGs or take the long way through EMF conversion, Google Drawings, and copy-paste.

SVG to Slides lets you drag the original file straight into your deck.

Your SVGs, with all the flexibility of native Slides shapes.

Edit individual elements.

Every SVG path becomes its own Slides shape. Select the palm without touching the wordmark, then recolour it with the standard fill picker.

Resize without losing quality.

From a small footer icon to a full-slide graphic, every edge stays sharp.

Use SVGs in your themes.

Add converted graphics to your theme layouts and reuse them across slides. The native shapes also work in Google Drawings.
