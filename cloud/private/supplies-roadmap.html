01 · Set up

Start every deck at the right size, on brand.

Print, screen and social formats

Page Sizes

Concept

You know you need a letter one-pager or a LinkedIn banner. You don’t know the dimensions. Page Sizes adds named presets to Slides’ own Page setup dialog, grouped into print, screen, social and cards, and fills in the width and height for you.

For print, toggle bleed, safe margins and crop marks, and they land on the master before you place anything. Slides only asks for a custom width and height; Page Sizes adds the marks a printer expects.

Branded themes from base layouts

Theme Kits

Concept

A Slides theme starts from a blank master. Theme Kits starts from plain, well-built base layouts for title, section, content, stat and closing slides, then applies your font pairing, palette and logo in four steps.

The logo comes in through the SVG to Slides pipeline as native shapes, so it stays sharp and takes theme colours.

02 · Bring in

Get vectors and data in as shapes you can edit.

Vector logos and icons

SVG to Slides

Live

Slides has no SVG import, so logos end up as flattened PNGs or take a detour through EMF and Google Drawings™. Drag an SVG onto an open deck, click to place it, press ⌘ + V, and every path arrives as a native Slides shape you can recolour and resize without losing quality.

Conversion runs in your browser. The extension asks for no Google permissions, and the file stays on your device.

Charts from Google Sheets™ data

Vector Charts

Concept

Pick a range in Google Sheets™ and the chart draws in the deck as vector shapes, with every label a real Slides text box. Recolour one bar, retype one label, or restyle every axis at once through Classes.

A linked Sheets chart arrives as a single picture. Vector Charts runs on the same converter as SVG to Slides, so each bar is its own shape.

Place things with the precision of a design tool.

Exact alignment and spacing

Align & Space

Concept

Partner logos, team cards and process rows look wrong when each gap is a few points off. Align & Space sets the gap in points, aligns to a key object, and tidies a loose selection into a row or grid, with distances shown while you drag.

Slides distributes objects evenly between the outer two. Align & Space sets the gap itself, so the same 24 pt spacing holds from slide 3 to slide 30.

Photo collages

Auto Grid

Concept

Drop a pile of photos on a slide and choose a layout: even grid, justified rows, mosaic or tilted scatter. Drag across the slide to widen or tighten the gap, and shuffle until the composition works.

Slides has no layout engine for images. Auto Grid arranges the photos already placed and leaves ordinary images behind.

04 · Repeat

Do it once, then apply it everywhere.

Styles across the whole deck

Classes

Concept

A new brand font means clicking through every slide to change each headline. Classes finds every element that shares a font, colour, size or shape type, saves the match as H1, Body or Caption, and applies one edit to all of them.

Google Docs™ has paragraph styles; Slides does not. Vector Charts and Theme Kits tag what they create with the same classes.

Keyboard shortcuts for any menu

Shortcuts

Built

Aligning, distributing, Paint format and PDF export only exist as menu clicks in Slides. Shortcuts loads a curated Ctrl + Alt set for deck-building, binds any menu item to a key, and maps every binding on a visual keyboard.

Each combo is checked against Slides, Chrome and macOS before you save it. Bindings live in Chrome sync storage, and nothing is sent anywhere.
